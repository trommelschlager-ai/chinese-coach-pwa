# v94 Game scenery and reuse notes

The images in this revision are locally hosted illustrations used behind the interactive game UI. They are not screenshots and contain no interface text. The local WebP derivatives are cropped/resized for mobile screens.

- `assets/game-scene-build-v94.webp` is adapted from **Ai Generated, Pagoda, Cherry Blossom** by ciaorioris: https://pixabay.com/illustrations/ai-generated-pagoda-cherry-blossom-8119677/
- `assets/game-scene-choose-v94.webp` is adapted from **Ai Generated, Pagoda, Yard** by Kyraxys / Auvryon: https://pixabay.com/illustrations/ai-generated-pagoda-yard-japan-8563197/

Both sources were marked **Free for use under the Pixabay Content License** when selected on November 1, 2026. The images are stylistic East-Asian fantasy illustrations rather than historically accurate depictions of Chinese buildings. No other lesson images were modified.
