# Local learning media

Word recordings in `assets/course-human/` come from [hugolpz/audio-cmn](https://github.com/hugolpz/audio-cmn), `64k/hsk/`. Speaker: Yue Tan. License: CC BY-SA 3.0 US, as documented by the original SWAC / Shtooka collection. Original source links and attribution remain in the app settings. Files are redistributed without audio edits.

The isolated `mā` recording (`cmn-妈.mp3`) comes from that repository's `64k/syllabs/cmn-ma1.mp3`. Speaker: Chen Wang, CC BY-SA, per the source repository's authorship table. It is an isolated syllable, not a concatenated phrase.

The existing ten character recordings in `assets/hanzi-human/` retain their existing Yue Tan attribution. Listening examples are now selected from the local word and character recordings. Existing Wikimedia voice demos retain their individual on-screen credits.

Words with an exact local recording play that recording. Sentences without an exact recording continue to use the installed Mandarin voice, explicitly labeled in the player. No separate syllables are concatenated to simulate connected speech. Acoustic and tone validation of the complete audio collection has not been performed in this update.

`assets/vendor/hanzi-writer.min.js` is Hanzi Writer 3.7.3, copyright David Chanin, MIT; the full license is in `assets/vendor/hanzi-writer-LICENSE.txt`.

The ten JSON files in `assets/hanzi-data/` come from hanzi-writer-data 2.0.1 / Make Me a Hanzi, based on Arphic Technology font data. The full Arphic Public License is in `assets/hanzi-data/ARPHICPL.TXT`. Sources: https://github.com/chanind/hanzi-writer-data and https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi.
